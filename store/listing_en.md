**Full description** (≤4000 chars)
```
A new nonogram (picture logic / griddler) puzzle every single day — the same one for every
player, worldwide. Fill in the grid using the row and column number clues, and watch a picture
reveal itself. Solve it to keep your streak alive.

WHY NONOGRAM DAILY
• One guaranteed-solvable puzzle daily, generated fresh — no two days repeat, no guessing
  required if you reason it through.
• Full archive of every past puzzle, always playable, at zero extra storage cost.
• Free Play mode for endless puzzles at any size or difficulty, any time.
• Streaks track your longest and current runs, with unlockable colour themes as milestones.
• No account, no login, no cloud sync required — everything runs on your device.
• Colourblind-safe palette option and large, comfortable touch targets.

HOW TO PLAY
Each row and column carries clues describing the lengths of its filled runs. Tap to fill a
cell, tap again (or use the Mark tool) to cross it out. Long-press and drag to paint a whole
run at once. Use logic, not luck — every daily puzzle is checked to be solvable without
guesswork before it ever reaches you.

FREE TO PLAY
Nonogram Daily is free, supported by optional ads: watch one for a hint, an extra life, or an
extra archive puzzle — entirely your choice, never forced into the daily ritual.

Perfect for fans of picross, griddlers, hanjie, and picture cross puzzles who want a daily
habit that respects their time and their data.
```
